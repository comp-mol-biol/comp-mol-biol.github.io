Lukas Stelzl gave a talk as keynote speaker about the exciting ongoing work in the lab at the [Peptide Self-Assembly (PSA) Conference 2026](https://www.psacon2026.com/) in Mainz, Germany (10–12 August 2026). The conference brought together researchers working on self-assembling peptide systems, and Lukas presented our lab's latest work using molecular simulations to understand peptide and protein self-assembly.
