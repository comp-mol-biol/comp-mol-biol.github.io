Members of the lab attended the [Hünfeld Workshop on Computer Simulation and Theory of Macromolecules](https://www.mpinat.mpg.de/workshop/huenfeld), held at Hünfeld Monastery in Germany (24–25 April 2026). This year's meeting marked the workshop's 25th anniversary, bringing together researchers to discuss recent advances in the theory and application of biomolecular simulations.
