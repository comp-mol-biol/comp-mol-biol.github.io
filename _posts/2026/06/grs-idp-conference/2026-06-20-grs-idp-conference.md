Emanuele Zippo and Lukas Stelzl attended the Gordon Research Seminar "[Decoding Disorder by the Numbers: Quantitative and Integrative Approaches to IDPs Across Scales](https://www.grc.org/intrinsically-disordered-proteins-grs-conference/2026/)" in Les Diablerets, Switzerland (20–21 June 2026). The meeting brought together researchers using quantitative and computational approaches to study intrinsically disordered proteins and was a great opportunity to discuss ongoing research with the IDP community.
